TROPICAL HUNTS: every piece of on-screen text (tutorial + level 1), 2026-10-08
The screenshots are 1920x1080: the game is 480x270 pixels scaled up 4x. All text is a hand-made 5x7 pixel font
(capitals only, plus a lower-case "s"), drawn in BOOM's palette. {Braces} below = the highlighted (yellow) word.

TUTORIAL (folder 1)
  Intro banner:        TUTORIAL / LEARN EVERY MOVE, ONE AT A TIME
  Station titles:      01 MOVE, 02 JUMP, 03 DOUBLE JUMP, 04 SLASH, 05 SMASH, 06 CHARGED SMASH, 07 COUNTER,
                       08 THUNDERCLAP FLASH  (slam in big in the middle, then shrink to the top)
  Key band (bottom):   arrow keys / SPACE / SPACE THEN SPACE / Q OR HOLD Q / W / W + charge meter / Q / double-tap →
  Clear words:         NICE!, CLEAR!, GREAT!, JUICY!, CLEAR!, BOOM!, PERFECT!, LIGHTNING!   (then GO >>> top right)
  Tips (wrong move):   STAND STILL AND PRESS {Q}
                       STAND STILL AND PRESS {W}
                       HOLD {W} DOWN UNTIL IT BLASTS
                       ALMOST! STAND ON THE COLUMN AND HOLD {W} LONGER
                       TOO EARLY! WAIT FOR THE LUNGE, THEN PRESS {Q}
                       TOO LATE! PRESS {Q} WHILE IT'S IN THE AIR
                       DON'T ATTACK: GALLOP INTO THEM
                       OUCH! LET GO TO STOP BEFORE YOU HIT A WALL
  In the world:        STAND HERE (arrow) -> NOW HOLD {W} (green), "!" over the Mango, NOW! + Q key
  Finale:              TUTORIAL COMPLETE! / NOW GO HUNT SOME FRUIT! / PRESS {ENTER} FOR LEVEL 1 (not in the shots)

LEVEL HUD (folder 2)
  Level title:         LEVEL 1 / JUNGLE RUN
  Juice clock:         20.0 counting down (top centre), +1s / +3s (green), -5s (red), red pulse under 10s
  Toasts:              CHECKPOINT!   OOPS!   OUT OF JUICE!
  Over enemies:        NO DAMAGE   IMMUNE IN WATER   (grey)
  Boss:                PINA COLADA (name slam, then name + health bar)
  Fail card:           THANKS FOR PLAYING! / PRESS {ENTER} TO TRY AGAIN
  Level clear:         LEVEL CLEAR! / TIME 1:23.4 / RANK S (or A, B, C) / THANKS FOR PLAYING!
                       (other levels: THE END! ... PRESS {ENTER} TO CONTINUE)

COMBO AND COUNTER (folder 3)
  Combo counter:       N HITS, ranks NICE (5), GREAT (10), JUICY! (20), TROPICAL!! (35), FRESH SQUEEZED (50)
  Counter:             COUNTER! with X2, X3 ... under it
  Prompts:             Q key + COUNTER! (the dam flood), W key + SLAM! (the dynamite throw)
  Kills:               +1 (small, over Mangos killed by cactus spikes / the palm)

DIALOGUE (folder 4): speech bubbles, her = orange line, you = teal line
  Opening
    HER: HEY! I'M REALLY FEELING LIKE A {PINA COLADA} RIGHT NOW.
    HER: IT WOULD BE SO NICE IF YOU COULD DO THAT FOR ME ♥
    YOU: WELL... I'M OUT OF STOCK. BUT FOR YOU... I'LL SEE WHAT I CAN DO.
    HER: OH THANK YOU SWEETIE! I KNEW YOU'D BE A DARLING. BUT MAKE IT FAST, I'M SO THIRSTY!
    HER: ...SO I'M WILLING TO WAIT ABOUT 20 SECONDS.   (the 20 is red, shakes, then flies up to become the clock)
    YOU: I NEED TO GO FIGHT... UHH, I MEAN... *FIND* THE RIGHT JUICES.
    YOU: I'LL BE RIGHT BACK

    YOU: SORRY... I RAN OUT OF JUICE. I COULDN'T DO IT.
    HER: AWW... AND I WAS SO THIRSTY, TOO...

    YOU: ONE {PINA COLADA}, FRESHLY SQUEEZED!
    HER: MMM... IT'S GREAT! YOU'RE AWESOME, SWEETIE ♥
